Questions

What practices ask first.

Straight answers on patient data, GDPR and which tools to use.

Can we use AI with patient data?

Not in a public chatbot. Never paste names, NHS numbers or clinical notes into one. The course shows you how to write messages with gaps like [name], so your practice software fills them in.

What about UK GDPR?

Health data needs extra care under UK GDPR. The course covers what is safe to share and how to check a tool’s settings. It is guidance, not legal advice.

Which AI tools does it use?

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and Copilot, next to the software you already use. The free versions are enough to start.

Will it replace our receptionists?

No. It takes the repetitive writing off their desk, so they have more time for patients.

How long does it take?

Each lesson takes 5 to 10 minutes. Module 1 is ready in under a minute.
