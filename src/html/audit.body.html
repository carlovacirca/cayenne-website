Questions

Fair questions, straight answers.

If the numbers say AI is not worth it for your business, we will tell you on the call.

Is it really free?

Yes. The call is free, with nothing to pay later. Some owners ask us to build the ideas for them afterwards. That is entirely up to you.

What do I need to tell you?

Just your business name, town and a phone number. Anything else is optional. We never sell or share your details, and you can ask us to delete them at any time.

How long does it take?

About a minute to book, then a 30-minute call at the time you pick.

Will you try to sell me something?

No. We show you what you can do yourself with the free course, and what we could build if you would rather not. After the call, nobody chases you.

What if AI is not right for my business?

Then we will tell you. If the hours saved are too small to bother with, we say so plainly.
