Do we need to be good with tech?

No. We start with what your team already does on their phones and build from there. Nobody gets left behind.

Which AI tools do you teach?

The ones that suit you, usually ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot. Often it is one you already pay for.

Where does the training happen?

At your place or online. Sessions are short and booked around your opening hours.

How much does it cost?

Your price comes with your free audit. It depends on the size of your team and what you want to learn.

Is our information safe?

Yes. We show your team what is fine to put into AI, what is not, and the settings that keep your data private.
