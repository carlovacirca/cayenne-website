How to write quotes with ChatGPT

Turn rough notes into a clear, friendly quote in ten minutes. Every price stays yours.

In this guide
  1. 1Jot down the job
  2. 2Ask for a first draft
  3. 3Put in your prices
  4. 4Make it sound like you
  5. 5Save your template
  6. Before you send
6 min read · 2 prompts

A good quote wins the job before you have picked up a tool. It is clear, it is friendly and it says exactly what is included. ChatGPT is very good at the clear and friendly part. You stay in charge of the prices and the promises.

  • You need
  • Your job notes
  • Free ChatGPT account
  • Ten minutes

1Jot down the job in your own words

Write it the way you would explain it to someone on your team: what the job is, what is included, how long it takes and anything to watch out for. Bullet points are fine. Spelling does not matter.

Leave out the customer’s full name, phone number and address. You add those yourself at the very end, so they never go into the chat.

2Ask ChatGPT for a first draft

Open ChatGPT and start a new chat. Copy the prompt below, paste it in, then paste your notes where it says and press send.

Prompt 1 · The first draft
Turn my notes below into a clear, friendly quote for a customer.

Use these headings: The job, What is included, What is not included, Timings, Price, Next steps.

Write [price] wherever a price goes. Do not add any prices or totals yourself.

Keep it under 250 words, in plain UK English, with short sentences.

My notes:
[paste your notes here]

In a few seconds you get a tidy quote with clear headings. Read it through once. If something is wrong, say so in plain words, just as you would to a person.

For exampleThe skip is part of the job, not an extra. Move it into What is included.

3Put in your own prices

Wherever the quote says [price], type your own figure. Never ask ChatGPT what to charge. It does not know your costs, your area or how busy you are.

If you charge VAT, show it the way you always do, so the customer sees the same layout as every quote you have sent before.

4Make it sound like you

A first draft can sound a little stiff. This second prompt changes only the tone. Every price, date and heading stays exactly where you put it.

Prompt 2 · In your voice
Rewrite this quote so it sounds like me.

I run a small [type of business] in [your town]. I am friendly, honest and straight to the point.

Use short sentences and everyday words. Keep every heading, every price and every date exactly as it is.

Here is the quote:
[paste the quote here]

5Save it as your template

Copy the finished quote into your usual document or quoting app, then add the customer’s name and address. Save a blank copy too. Next time, the whole job takes about five minutes.

Tip

Keep one chat just for quotes. Paste your best quote in first and say: Use this layout and tone for every new job. Then each new quote starts from your best one.

Check before you send

0 of 5 checked

Tick each one off. It takes a minute and saves an awkward phone call later.
