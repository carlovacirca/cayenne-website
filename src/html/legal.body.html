Part 1 of 3

Your privacy.

In short
  • We only collect what we need to run the course, the audit and our services.
  • We never sell your details or share them for anyone else’s marketing.
  • You can see, correct or delete your details at any time.

1Who we are

Cayenne helps UK businesses learn and use AI. In this notice, “we” and “us” means Cayenne company name, number and address to be confirmed. We are the controller of your personal data, which means we decide how it is used.

2What we collect

  • Your business name, type and town, when you build a course or ask for an audit.
  • The goals and answers you give us, such as how many enquiries you get each week.
  • Your name and email address, if you create an account or send us a message.
  • Basic technical details, such as your browser and the pages you visit, but only if you allow analytics cookies.

We never ask for passwords to your own systems, and we do not take payments on this site.

3How we use it

  • To write your personalised course and your AI audit.
  • To reply to your messages and look after your account.
  • To improve the site, using totals rather than tracking you as a person wherever we can.
  • To email you about Cayenne, only if you have said yes. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe.

The law asks us to name our reasons. They are: to give you what you asked for (contract), to run our business in ways you would expect (legitimate interests), and your consent, for marketing emails and analytics cookies.

4AI tools and your details

Some features use AI services to write your course or your audit. We send them only what that job needs, never passwords or bank details. We only use providers who agree not to train their AI models on what we send. Provider list to be confirmed

5Who we share it with

A small number of suppliers help us host the site, send emails and run AI features. They may only use your details to provide that service to us. If any of them store data outside the UK, we make sure the safeguards UK law requires are in place. We never sell your details.

6How long we keep it

  • Account and course details: while your account is open, then for 12 months.
  • Messages you send us: 2 years.
  • Audit answers: 2 years, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

7Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see the details we hold about you, correct them, delete them or take a copy with you. You can also object to how we use them, or withdraw your consent at any time. Ask through our contact page and we will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Our cookies.

In short
  • Essential cookies keep the site working. These are always on.
  • Analytics cookies stay off until you say yes.
  • We do not use advertising cookies.

1What cookies are

2Changing your mind

You can change your choice at any time in cookie settings, or clear cookies in your browser. Turning off essential cookies may stop parts of the site working, such as signing in.

Terms of use.

In short
  • The course and the AI audit are free. No card, no catch.
  • AI can be wrong, so check anything important before you rely on it.
  • Paid work always comes with a written agreement and a price agreed first.

1About these terms

These terms apply when you use the Cayenne website, the free course and the free AI audit. By using them, you agree to these terms.

2Your account

Keep your sign-in details safe, and tell us if you think someone else has used your account. You need to be 18 or over to create one.

3The course and the audit

We write them around your business, using the details you give us. They are general guidance, not legal, financial or medical advice. Figures in your audit, such as hours you could save, are estimates.

4Using what AI writes

AI tools can make mistakes. Before you send anything AI has written to a customer, or rely on it for a decision, check it. That part is always your call.

5Paid services

If you ask us to build something, we agree the work and the price in writing before we start. That agreement sits alongside these terms and takes priority if the two ever differ.

6Who owns what

Anything you give us stays yours, and we only use it to provide what you asked for. The course material and the words and design of this site belong to Cayenne. Use what you learn in your own business as much as you like, but please do not copy or resell the course.
